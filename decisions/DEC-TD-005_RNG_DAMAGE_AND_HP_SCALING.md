# DEC-TD-005 — RNG damage range and HP scaling

**Status:** APPROVED  
**Approved by:** Owner  
**Date:** 2026-08-26

## Damage ranges

Expected damage remains unchanged while the min/max range widens with each Damage level:

| Level | Damage | Expected value |
|---|---:|---:|
| Base | 24–30 | 27 |
| Damage L1 | 31–39 | 35 |
| Damage L2 | 38–48 | 43 |
| Damage L3 | 45–57 | 51 |

## Creep HP scaling

- Wave 1 remains **60 HP**.
- Authored base creep HP increases by **+8 HP per wave**, replacing +10.

Wave 30 authored HP is therefore 292 instead of 350, before approved creep mutations.

## Constraints

- Do not change prices, interest, rewards, wave cadence, lap duration, firing sector, overload cap, or any other combat value in this decision.
- Measure full-spend purchase trajectories and intelligent multi-target Overdrive use through wave 30 before proposing a further number change.
