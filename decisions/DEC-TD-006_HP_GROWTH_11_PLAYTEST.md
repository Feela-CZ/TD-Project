# DEC-TD-006 — +11 HP-per-wave playtest baseline

**Status:** APPROVED  
**Approved by:** Owner  
**Date:** 2026-08-26

## Decision

Set authored base creep HP growth to **+11 HP per wave**. Wave 1 remains 60 HP.

This supersedes the +8 HP-per-wave default in DEC-TD-005 for the playable alpha and default simulator.

## Known measured risk

With the current four deterministic full-spend trajectories, the +11 experiment completed wave 30 in 0/30 runs. This decision is therefore a deliberately hard playtest baseline, not a certification that the stated "hard but possible" wave-30 target is met.

## Follow-up under investigation

Evaluate integer-only nonlinear authored HP growth as a possible way to preserve demanding early/mid-game pressure while returning a disciplined strategy to wave-30 viability. No nonlinear formula is approved by this decision.
