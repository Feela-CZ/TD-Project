# DEC-TD-008 — English client and Tempo Charge experiment

**Status:** Approved by Owner

## English client

All future playable Tower Defense builds use English player-facing text. Owner
communication and project-direction material remain Czech. The existing Czech
alpha is not partially translated; the next playable iteration receives one
coherent English localisation pass.

## Tempo Charge experiment

The next playable iteration introduces Tempo Charge as an experimental active
mechanic. A player earns one charge after clearing every creep from their own
field once that Incursion's normal deployment has completed. A player can hold
at most one charge; a later clear while one is stored or queued creates no
second reserve.

When used, the charge queues the next opponent Incursion to start as soon as
the opponent's current Incursion has finished its normal spawn window. The
accelerated Incursion is the same numbered future Incursion that would
otherwise arrive at the next shared wave time; no extra Incursions are added.
At that normal shared time, the opponent does not receive another copy, and
both sides return to the ordinary shared sequence on the following Incursion.

The mechanic remains subject to playtest and may be retuned or removed if it
does not create useful strategic pressure.
