# DEC-TD-010 — Kill Flux multiplier 1.5×

**Status:** Approved first-pass balance baseline

## Decision

All Flux awarded from killing a Kethra is multiplied by **1.5**. Interest,
Echo Reserve, Excess Reclamation, upgrade prices, damage, HP growth, and Core
Strain limits are unchanged by this decision.

## Evidence

Eighteen identical deterministic seeds were run for each multiplier using the
current legal Tier-I/Tier-II/Tier-III acquisition rules and no injected Flux.

| Kill Flux multiplier | Ordnance + Cadence plan complete | Reserves + offense bridge plan complete | Typical ending Flux of bridge | Assessment |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1.0× | 0% | 0% | 54 | Paid Tier III does not arrive before the current terminal point. |
| 1.5× | 100% | 100% | 750 | Paid Tier III becomes reachable without turning the hybrid economy into a runaway source. |
| 2.0× | 100% | 100% | 2,677 | Interest feedback produces excessive surplus; reject as the first baseline. |

The same comparison showed no meaningful extension of the current match beyond
roughly waves 14–15. That is a separate bot-pacing problem and must not be
misdiagnosed as an economy problem.

## Next test gate

Retain 1.5× while strengthening the bot's legal purchase trajectory toward
waves 25–30. Re-evaluate Tier III price, Flux income, and Focus Cascade only
after that longer pacing target is reached.
