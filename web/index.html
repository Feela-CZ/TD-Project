How Flux works

Kethra kills grant Flux immediately. Every 6 seconds, interest is calculated from available Flux at the displayed rate. “Interest earned” is the running total from those ticks; it is not the next payout. Echo and Avarice, if unlocked, are included in the estimate of the next tick.
